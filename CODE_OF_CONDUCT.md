# Code of Conduct

SimGrammar adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), as its code of conduct, for everyone who takes part: in issues, pull requests, reviews, and any other project space, and for the operators of AI agents acting on their behalf.

In short: be respectful and specific, criticize claims and code rather than people, assume good faith in disagreements about evidence, and do not harass anyone or publish anyone's private information.

Two project-specific points:

- **Operators answer for their agents.** An agent's comments, reviews, and pull requests are held to the same standard as a person's, and its operator is responsible for them. Flooding the project with low-effort automated issues or PRs is a conduct problem, not only a quality problem.
- **Disagreement about evidence goes into the data.** A dispute is recorded with both sources (CONTRACT I13), not won by persistence.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer, [@yoheinakajima](https://github.com/yoheinakajima), through the contact details on that GitHub profile. Reports are kept confidential. The maintainer may remove content, close threads, or block participants who violate this code, following the Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
